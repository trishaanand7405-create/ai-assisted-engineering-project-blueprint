# Sprint 8 — Maintenance & Enhancement

> **Treat the released product as a living system that must be monitored, maintained and improved.**

Sprint 8 completes the simulated product lifecycle.

The team moves from **"We released it"** to:

> **"How will we keep it useful, reliable and ready for change?"**

---

## Sprint Goal

> **Define the product's post-release maintenance approach and implement one meaningful enhancement.**

---

## Inputs

Bring forward:

* Released product from Sprint 7
* Product Vision
* Product Goals
* Product Backlog
* Release information
* Known limitations
* User feedback
* Technical debt
* Retrospective observations

---

## 00–10 min — Plan

The team identifies:

* One important maintenance concern.
* One useful enhancement.
* Why each matters.
* What can realistically be completed during the Sprint.

Possible maintenance concerns:

* Defects
* Data quality
* Performance
* Reliability
* Security
* Dependency changes
* Model degradation
* Agent behaviour
* Simulation accuracy

---

## 10–45 min — Execute

### 1. Maintenance

Address one important post-release concern.

Examples:

* Fix a defect.
* Improve error handling.
* Improve data validation.
* Update a dependency.
* Improve reliability.
* Add monitoring information.
* Document an operational issue.

### 2. Enhancement

Implement one small but meaningful improvement based on:

* User feedback
* Product goals
* New requirements
* Observed limitations
* Retrospective learning

### 3. Update the Backlog

Add newly discovered work.

Classify items where useful:

``
BUG
TECHNICAL DEBT
ENHANCEMENT
NEW FEATURE
IMPROVEMENT
```

---

## 45–55 min — Final Review

Demonstrate:

1. Released product
2. Maintenance improvement
3. New enhancement
4. Updated backlog
5. Evidence of improvement

Then explain:

* What changed?
* Why was it needed?
* What remains?
* What would the team do next?

---

## 55–60 min — Final Retrospective

Reflect on the complete eight-Sprint journey.

Discuss:

* What did we learn about Agile?
* What did we learn about engineering?
* What did we learn about collaboration?
* Where did AI assistance help?
* Where did human judgement matter?
* What would we do differently on the next product?

Each team should identify **three lessons learned**.

---

## Sprint Deliverables

By the end of Sprint 8:

* [ ] Maintenance activity
* [ ] One meaningful enhancement
* [ ] Updated Product Backlog
* [ ] Final product demonstration
* [ ] Known technical debt
* [ ] Future improvement opportunities
* [ ] Three lessons learned

---

## Definition of Done for Sprint 8

Sprint 8 is Done when:

> **The team can demonstrate the released product, show how it was improved, and explain how the product could continue evolving.**

---

## Final Workshop Principle

> **Deployment is not the end of engineering. It is the beginning of the product's next lifecycle.**

``
IDEA
  ↓
PRODUCT
  ↓
ENGINEERING
  ↓
RELEASE
  ↓
MAINTENANCE
  ↓
FEEDBACK
  ↓
ENHANCEMENT
  ↺
```

**Build → Release → Learn → Improve → Repeat**
