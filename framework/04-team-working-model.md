# Team Working Model

> **A small, cross-functional team collaborates to deliver one product outcome.**

The workshop uses Agile teams of approximately **six students**.

The team structure is designed to simulate a real product engineering team while remaining simple enough for a short workshop.

---

## 1. Core Roles

Each team has three primary role areas:

### Product Owner

Focuses on **value and priorities**.

Responsibilities:

* Understand users and problems
* Maintain Product Vision
* Prioritise the Product Backlog
* Clarify User Stories
* Represent product value
* Accept completed work

### Scrum Master

Focuses on **team effectiveness and Agile process**.

Responsibilities:

* Facilitate Sprint activities
* Maintain time-boxes
* Identify impediments
* Encourage collaboration
* Support Reviews and Retrospectives
* Protect the team's focus

### Engineer / DevOps Engineer

Focuses on **technical delivery and product operation**.

Responsibilities:

* Solution design
* Implementation
* Integration
* Testing
* Deployment
* Technical decisions
* Operational considerations

---

## 2. Six-Member Team

The three primary roles do not mean that only three students perform the work.

The remaining team members contribute according to the current Sprint needs.

For example:

``
             PRODUCT OWNER
                   │
       ┌───────────┼───────────┐
       ↓           ↓           ↓
   ENGINEER    ENGINEER    ENGINEER
       │           │           │
       └────── SCRUM MASTER ────┘
```

The exact distribution of technical responsibilities may change from Sprint to Sprint.

---

## 3. Shared Responsibility

Everyone contributes to:

* Understanding the product
* Planning
* Problem solving
* Testing
* Documentation
* Demonstration
* Retrospective
* Continuous improvement

> **A role is an area of accountability, not a restriction on contribution.**

---

## 4. Collaboration Rules

Teams should:

* Communicate frequently.
* Make decisions visible.
* Ask for help early.
* Review important work.
* Integrate work continuously.
* Avoid isolated work for long periods.
* Keep the Sprint Goal visible.

When a team member becomes blocked, the team should help resolve the blocker rather than simply waiting.

---

## 5. Decision Ownership

Different decisions have different owners.

| Decision                        | Primary Owner |
| ------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Product priority                | Product Owner |
| Sprint facilitation             | Scrum Master  |
| Technical approach              | Engineering   |
| Acceptance of product increment | Product Owner |
| Team working improvement        | Entire Team   |

Important decisions should still be discussed collaboratively.

---

## 6. Team Behaviour

Effective teams demonstrate:

**Ownership**

The team takes responsibility for its product.

**Transparency**

Progress, problems and limitations are visible.

**Collaboration**

Members help one another achieve the Sprint Goal.

**Adaptability**

The team responds to new information.

**Accountability**

Each person follows through on commitments.

---

## Team Principle

> **Roles create clarity. Collaboration creates delivery.**

The team succeeds when the **product succeeds**, not when individual tasks are merely completed.
