# Sprints

> **Eight one-hour Sprints. One complete product lifecycle.**

This folder contains the Sprint playbooks for the AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint.

The workshop uses short, time-boxed Sprints to simulate how a professional Agile engineering team moves a product from **idea to release and continuous improvement**.

---

## Sprint Journey

The eight Sprints form one continuous product lifecycle:

| Sprint | Focus                     | Primary Outcome                      |
| ------ | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1      | Product Discovery         | Problem, users and product direction |
| 2      | Product Definition        | Vision, goals, stories and backlog   |
| 3      | Solution Design           | Buildable technical solution         |
| 4      | Implementation            | Working product increment            |
| 5      | Integration & Testing     | Tested end-to-end flow               |
| 6      | Working Product           | Usable product experience            |
| 7      | Deployment & Release      | Release-ready product                |
| 8      | Maintenance & Enhancement | Improved product and future backlog  |

---

## Common Sprint Rhythm

Every Sprint follows the same basic one-hour rhythm:

```text
10 min  → PLAN
35 min  → EXECUTE
10 min  → REVIEW
 5 min  → RETROSPECT
```

The rhythm creates consistency while the work changes from Sprint to Sprint.

---

## Continuous Product Evolution

The team does not start again at every Sprint.

Each Sprint builds on the previous Sprint:

```text
Sprint N
   ↓
Working Increment
   ↓
Review
   ↓
Feedback
   ↓
Updated Backlog
   ↓
Sprint N+1
```

The product should become progressively more useful, reliable and demonstrable.

## What Makes This a Simulation?

The purpose is not merely to complete eight classroom exercises.

Teams should experience:

**Product decisions → Prioritisation → Engineering → Collaboration → Time pressure → Demonstration → Feedback → Change**

The product should evolve throughout the workshop.

---

## Sprint Principle

> **Every Sprint must move the product forward and leave visible evidence of progress.**

The final objective is not perfect software.

It is to experience the discipline of **building, releasing and continuously improving a product as an Agile engineering team.**
