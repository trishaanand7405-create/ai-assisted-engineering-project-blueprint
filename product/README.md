# Product

> **Define the problem, understand the users, create the vision, establish goals, and turn value into an actionable backlog.**

This folder defines the **Product Management foundation** used by the AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint.

The Product discipline answers:

> **What are we building, for whom, why does it matter, and what should we build next?**

## Recommended Reading Order

Read the documents in sequence:

1. **[Problem Statement](01-problem-statement.md)**
   Define the problem, its impact, desired outcome and initial scope.

2. **[Users & Personas](02-users-and-personas.md)**
   Identify the people who experience the problem and the value they need.

3. **[Product Vision](03-product-vision.md)**
   Connect the problem and users to a clear product direction.

4. **[Product Goals](04-product-goals.md)**
   Translate the Vision into meaningful outcomes that can be evaluated.

5. **[User Stories](05-user-stories.md)**
   Translate product goals into user-centred, testable capabilities.

6. **[Product Backlog](06-product-backlog.md)**
   Organise, prioritise and continuously evolve the work required to improve the product.

## Product Flow

```text
PROBLEM
   ↓
USERS
   ↓
PRODUCT VISION
   ↓
PRODUCT GOALS
   ↓
USER STORIES
   ↓
PRODUCT BACKLOG
   ↓
SPRINTS
```

The Product Backlog then connects directly with the **Agile Sprint Framework**.

## Working Principle

The Product discipline is intentionally **lean**.

Teams should focus on understanding the problem, delivering user value and making informed decisions rather than producing unnecessary documentation.

> **Problem First → User First → Value First → Technology Later**

The Product Owner maintains product direction and priorities, while the entire team contributes to understanding, shaping and improving the product.
