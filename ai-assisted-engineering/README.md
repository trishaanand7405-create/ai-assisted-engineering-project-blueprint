# AI-Assisted Engineering

> **AI assists engineering. Engineers remain responsible for the result.**

This folder defines how AI can be used responsibly throughout the product engineering lifecycle.

The objective is **not** to teach students how to generate maximum AI output.

The objective is to teach them how to use AI to **increase engineering capability while preserving human judgement, verification and accountability**.

## AI Across the Lifecycle

AI assistance can be applied throughout the project:

``
PRODUCT DISCOVERY
       ↓
REQUIREMENTS
       ↓
SOLUTION DESIGN
       ↓
CODING
       ↓
TESTING
       ↓
DEPLOYMENT
       ↓
MAINTENANCE
       ↓
IMPROVEMENT
```

The level and type of AI assistance should change according to the task.

---

## The Human-in-the-Loop Model

The blueprint follows a simple rule:

``
AI GENERATES / SUGGESTS
          ↓
    HUMAN REVIEWS
          ↓
    HUMAN VALIDATES
          ↓
     TEAM DECIDES
          ↓
      PRODUCT USES
```

AI output is never automatically treated as correct.

## Responsible AI Principles

Teams should remember:

**Understand**

Know what the AI-generated output means.

**Verify**

Check important claims, code and results.

**Protect**

Do not expose confidential or sensitive information.

**Test**

Validate generated solutions with appropriate evidence.

**Own**

The team remains accountable for the final result.

---

## Clean, Green and Lean AI

AI itself can encourage unnecessary complexity and computation.

Teams should therefore ask:

* Do we actually need AI for this task?
* Can a simpler approach solve the problem?
* Are we generating unnecessary content?
* Are we introducing unnecessary dependencies?
* Are we consuming unnecessary compute resources?

> **Use AI where it creates value—not simply because AI is available.**

---

## Core Principle

> **AI can accelerate engineering. It cannot replace engineering responsibility.**

A successful AI-assisted engineering team is not the team that uses AI the most.

It is the team that uses AI **intelligently, responsibly and purposefully to deliver better products.**
