# Agile Product Lifecycle

> **From Product Vision to Working Product — and from Working Product to Continuous Improvement.**

This project follows an **Agile product lifecycle** in which the team continuously plans, builds, tests, demonstrates, learns and improves.

## 1. Product Vision

Define **what we are building, for whom, why it matters, and what value it should create**.

**Output:** Product Vision + Problem Statement + Initial Scope

---

## 2. Product Backlog

Translate the vision into a prioritised collection of **Epics, User Stories and Acceptance Criteria**.

The Product Owner owns the priority of the backlog.

**Output:** Prioritised Product Backlog

---

## 3. Sprint Planning

Select the highest-priority work that the team can realistically accomplish during the Sprint.

Define:

* Sprint Goal
* Sprint Backlog
* Expected Increment
* Definition of Done

**Output:** Sprint Goal + Sprint Backlog

---

## 4. Build the Increment

The engineering team designs, develops, integrates and tests the selected functionality.

The implementation depends on the project type:

* AI/ML/DS/DL application
* Intelligent data pipeline
* Multi-agentic pipeline
* Digital Twin

**Output:** Working Product Increment

---

## 5. Test & Validate

Verify that the increment:

* satisfies acceptance criteria
* works as expected
* produces meaningful results
* does not break existing functionality
* meets the team's Definition of Done

**Output:** Tested Increment

---

## 6. Sprint Review

Demonstrate the working increment to the Product Owner / stakeholders.

Collect feedback and identify:

* accepted functionality
* required changes
* new requirements
* defects
* enhancement opportunities

**Output:** Feedback + Updated Backlog

---

## 7. Retrospective

The team reflects on **how it worked**, not just what it built.

Discuss:

* What went well?
* What did not go well?
* What should we improve?
* What will we change in the next Sprint?

**Output:** Improvement Actions

---

## 8. Deployment & Usage

Make the product usable by its intended user.

Deployment may be **local, web-based, API-based or another appropriate form**. Cloud infrastructure is not required.

**Output:** Usable Product

---

## 9. Maintenance & Enhancement

A working product is **not the end of the lifecycle**.

Respond to:

* user feedback
* defects
* changing requirements
* new data
* performance issues
* new capabilities

These become new backlog items and enter subsequent Sprints.

**Output:** Improved Product Increment

---

# The Continuous Cycle

```text
Product Vision
      ↓
Product Backlog
      ↓
Sprint Planning
      ↓
Build
      ↓
Test
      ↓
Sprint Review
      ↓
Retrospective
      ↓
Deploy / Use
      ↓
Feedback
      ↓
Maintenance / Enhancement
      ↓
Updated Backlog
      ↓
Next Sprint
```

## Our Engineering Principle

> **Every Sprint should produce a demonstrable increment of value.**

Agile is not simply about working faster.

**Agile is about delivering value, receiving feedback, adapting to change, and continuously improving the product.**
