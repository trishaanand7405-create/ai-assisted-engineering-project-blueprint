# Checklists

> **Turn engineering discipline into repeatable action.**

This folder contains practical checklists for the major stages of the AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint.

The checklists are intentionally lightweight. They are not intended to replace engineering judgement, detailed procedures or project-specific documentation.

Their purpose is to help teams **pause, verify and make deliberate decisions** before moving through important lifecycle stages.

## How to Use These Checklists

A checklist should be used as a **decision-support tool**, not as a bureaucratic gate.

A team should:

1. Review the relevant checklist.
2. Mark items based on actual evidence.
3. Discuss important gaps.
4. Resolve critical issues where necessary.
5. Explicitly accept appropriate risks.
6. Make the readiness decision.

The objective is informed action.

---

## Checklist Status

The checklists use three practical outcomes where appropriate:

### Ready

The necessary conditions are sufficiently satisfied.

### Ready With Known Limitations

The team understands the remaining limitations and consciously accepts them.

### Not Ready

Important conditions remain unresolved or insufficiently understood.

## AI-Assisted Use

AI can help teams:

* Review checklist completeness
* Identify potentially overlooked items
* Explain checklist criteria
* Suggest evidence
* Summarise readiness gaps

However, checklist completion must be based on **actual project evidence**.

AI should not be used to manufacture readiness.

> **A checked box without evidence is only an assumption.**

---

## Avoid Checklist Theatre

A checklist becomes counterproductive when teams:

* Tick every item without discussion
* Treat every item as equally important
* Collect evidence that nobody uses
* Add unnecessary checks
* Use checklists instead of engineering judgement

The appropriate checklist should be **proportional to project complexity and risk**.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **Use checklists to make important questions visible, not to replace thinking.**

The purpose of this folder is simple:

**Pause → Check → Understand → Decide → Proceed.**
