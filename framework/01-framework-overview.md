# Framework Overview

> **One product. One team. One disciplined lifecycle.**

The AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint provides a common framework for teams to experience how modern engineering organisations transform a product idea into a working, released and continuously improving product.

The framework is intentionally **technology-independent**.

---

## 1. The Core Model

Every project follows the same fundamental flow:

``
PROBLEM
   ↓
PRODUCT
   ↓
AGILE
   ↓
ENGINEERING
   ↓
WORKING PRODUCT
   ↓
RELEASE
   ↓
FEEDBACK
   ↓
IMPROVEMENT
   ↺
```

The technology may change.

The engineering lifecycle does not.

---

## 2. The Five Core Dimensions

### Product

Defines:

* Problem
* Users
* Vision
* Goals
* User Stories
* Product Backlog

**Question:** What should we build and why?

### Agile

Defines:

* Roles
* Sprints
* Events
* Priorities
* Reviews
* Retrospectives

**Question:** How do we organise and evolve the work?

### Engineering

Defines:

* Design
* Data
* Components
* Implementation
* Testing
* Technical decisions

**Question:** How do we build it correctly?

### Project Track

Provides the technical context:

* AI / ML / DS / DL
* Intelligent Data Pipelines
* Multi-Agentic Pipelines
* Digital Twins

**Question:** What kind of technical product are we building?

### Sprints

Provide the execution rhythm.

**Question:** What should we accomplish next?

---

## 3. The Team Model

Every project is owned by an Agile team.

The team works collectively while maintaining clear responsibilities.

Core roles include:

* Product Owner
* Scrum Master
* Engineer / DevOps Engineer

Additional responsibilities may be distributed among team members as required by the project.

> **Roles provide accountability. They do not create silos.**

---

## 4. The Working Product

The objective of the framework is not documentation for its own sake.

Each major activity should contribute towards a working product.

``
IDEA
 ↓
DECISION
 ↓
DESIGN
 ↓
IMPLEMENTATION
 ↓
TEST
 ↓
WORKING INCREMENT
 ↓
RELEASE
 ↓
FEEDBACK
```

Documentation exists to support decisions, communication, reproducibility and learning.

---

## 5. AI-Assisted Engineering

AI tools may assist teams with:

* Research
* Analysis
* Design exploration
* Coding
* Testing
* Documentation
* Debugging
* Review

However:

> **AI generates assistance. Engineers provide judgement, verification and accountability.**

Teams must understand and validate what they use.

---

## 6. Engineering Philosophy

The framework follows three guiding principles:

### Clean

Keep the product, code, architecture and documentation understandable.

### Green

Avoid unnecessary computation, infrastructure, duplication and resource consumption.

### Lean

Build only what is needed to demonstrate meaningful product value.

> **Do the simplest useful thing, validate it, learn from it and improve it.**

---

## Framework Outcome

By following this framework, a team should be able to explain:

> **What problem are we solving?
> Who are we solving it for?
> What are we building?
> How are we building it?
> How do we know it works?
> How do we release it?
> How will we improve it?**

That is the essence of the AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint.
