# Agile Roles & Responsibilities

> **Three clear accountabilities. One collaborative team. One shared Product Goal.**

Each Agile team has **6 members** and operates with three defined accountability roles:

1. **Product Owner**
2. **Scrum Master**
3. **(DevOps) Engineer**

The roles establish **accountability**, not silos. All six team members collaborate throughout the product lifecycle.

---

## 1. Product Owner

### Primary Accountability

**Maximise product value.**

The Product Owner represents the product/user perspective and decides **what should be built and in what priority**.

### Responsibilities

* Define and communicate the **Product Vision**
* Understand the target users and their problems
* Define product goals and expected outcomes
* Create, maintain and prioritise the **Product Backlog**
* Define and clarify User Stories
* Define or validate Acceptance Criteria
* Answer product-related questions during development
* Review completed increments
* Accept or reject delivered functionality
* Collect and interpret user/stakeholder feedback
* Prioritise defects, changes and enhancements

### Key Question

> **Are we building the right product?**

---

## 2. Scrum Master

### Primary Accountability

**Enable effective Agile collaboration and Sprint execution.**

The Scrum Master helps the team follow the agreed Agile way of working and removes or escalates impediments.

### Responsibilities

* Facilitate Sprint Planning
* Help the team establish a clear Sprint Goal
* Facilitate Sprint Reviews
* Facilitate Retrospectives
* Maintain Sprint discipline and time-boxes
* Identify and help remove impediments
* Encourage effective communication
* Protect the team from unnecessary distractions
* Help maintain transparency of progress
* Encourage continuous improvement
* Ensure agreed Agile practices are followed

The Scrum Master **does not manage the team like a traditional project manager**.

### Key Question

> **Are we working effectively as an Agile team?**

---

## 3. (DevOps) Engineer

### Primary Accountability

**Turn the product requirements into a working, tested and usable product.**

The (DevOps) Engineer coordinates the technical implementation across the product lifecycle.

### Responsibilities

* Analyse technical requirements
* Contribute to solution architecture
* Select appropriate technologies and tools
* Design and implement the solution
* Manage data, models, agents, simulations or pipelines as applicable
* Integrate components
* Write and execute tests
* Diagnose and resolve technical issues
* Prepare the product for deployment
* Deploy or package the working product
* Support usage and maintenance
* Implement approved enhancements
* Maintain technical documentation

**DevOps does not require Docker, Kubernetes or cloud infrastructure for this workshop.**

Deployment means making the product **usable in an appropriate environment**.

### Key Question

> **Are we building the product correctly and making it usable?**

---

## Shared Team Responsibility

Regardless of role, every team member is expected to:

* communicate openly
* help other team members
* review work
* identify risks and impediments
* support testing and validation
* respect Sprint commitments
* learn continuously
* contribute to the final product

### The Three Accountability Questions

```text
PRODUCT OWNER
Are we building the right product?
            │
            ▼
SCRUM MASTER

            │
            ▼
(DEVOPS) ENGINEER

```

> **Roles create accountability. Collaboration creates the product.**
