# Sprint 1 — Product Discovery

> **Understand the problem before deciding what to build.**

Sprint 1 begins the product journey.

The team moves from a broad idea to a shared understanding of the **problem, users and desired outcome**.

Do not start implementation in this Sprint.

---

## Sprint Goal

> **Create a clear problem definition and establish the initial product direction.**

---

## Inputs

The team receives:

* Assigned Project Track
* Initial project context
* Team roles
* Workshop constraints

The team should use the Product and Agile guidance already provided in this blueprint.

---

## 00–10 min — Plan

The team should quickly agree on:

* Who is the Product Owner?
* Who is the Scrum Master?
* Who is responsible for engineering/DevOps activities?
* What problem are we trying to understand?
* Who experiences the problem?
* What evidence or assumptions do we currently have?

Record the key assumptions.

---

## 10–45 min — Execute

The team creates:

### 1. Problem Statement

Define:

* The problem
* Who experiences it
* Why it matters
* Current impact
* Desired improvement

### 2. Users / Personas

Identify the most important users or stakeholders.

For each major persona, identify:

* Who they are
* What they need
* Their primary pain point
* What successful outcome means to them

### 3. Initial Product Direction

Describe in simple terms what kind of product could address the problem.

Do not design the complete technical solution yet.

---

## 45–55 min — Review

The team presents:

1. Problem
2. Target users
3. Why the problem matters
4. Initial product idea
5. Key assumptions

The Product Owner should be able to explain the product idea in **approximately two minutes**.

---

## 55–60 min — Retrospective

Answer:

* What did we understand well?
* What remains uncertain?
* What assumption could be wrong?
* What should we improve in Sprint 2?

Record **one concrete improvement**.

---

## Sprint Deliverables

By the end of Sprint 1:

* [ ] Problem Statement
* [ ] Initial Users / Personas
* [ ] Initial Product Direction
* [ ] Key Assumptions
* [ ] Sprint Review evidence
* [ ] One Retrospective improvement

---

## Definition of Done for Sprint 1

Sprint 1 is Done when the team can clearly answer:

> **Who has the problem, what is the problem, why does it matter, and what kind of product might help?**

### Sprint Principle

> **Do not build the wrong product efficiently. Understand the problem first.**
