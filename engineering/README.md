# Engineering

> **Turn product intent into a working, tested and maintainable product through disciplined engineering.**

This folder defines the common **Engineering Workflow** used by all project teams in the AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint.

The Engineering discipline answers:

> **How do we turn a Product Backlog item into a working product increment?**

The same engineering principles apply across all four project tracks, even though their technologies and implementations may differ.

## Recommended Reading Order

Read the documents in sequence:

1. **[Engineering Workflow](01-engineering-workflow.md)**
   Understand the complete engineering cycle from requirement to improvement.

2. **[Solution Design](02-solution-design.md)**
   Translate requirements into a simple, implementable technical design.

3. **[Data & Components](03-data-and-components.md)**
   Understand inputs, data flows, components and their responsibilities.

4. **[Implementation](04-implementation.md)**
   Turn the design into a working product increment.

5. **[Testing & Validation](05-testing-and-validation.md)**
   Produce evidence that the implementation works as intended.

6. **[Engineering Decisions](06-engineering-decisions.md)**
   Record important technical decisions, reasoning and trade-offs.

## Engineering Flow

```text 
PRODUCT BACKLOG
       ↓
   UNDERSTAND
       ↓
      DESIGN
       ↓
      BUILD
       ↓
      TEST
       ↓
    INTEGRATE
       ↓
   DEMONSTRATE
       ↓
 LEARN & IMPROVE
       │
       └────────────→ NEXT SPRINT
```

## AI-Assisted Engineering

AI tools may assist throughout the engineering lifecycle.

However:

> **AI assists. Engineers decide, verify and take responsibility.**

Generated code, designs, analysis or recommendations must be reviewed and validated before becoming part of the product.

## Working Principle

Engineering in this blueprint is intentionally **clean, green and lean**.

Use the simplest solution that can reliably satisfy the requirement.

Avoid unnecessary complexity, infrastructure and documentation.

> **Understand → Design → Build → Test → Integrate → Demonstrate → Improve**
