# AI-Assisted Product Work

> **Use AI to explore possibilities, but let the product team decide what is valuable.**

AI can significantly accelerate product discovery and planning. It can help teams explore problems, users, requirements and alternatives before implementation begins.

The Product Owner remains accountable for product decisions.

---

## 1. Problem Discovery

AI can help the team:

* Explore a problem domain
* Identify possible user groups
* Analyse common pain points
* Generate questions for discovery
* Identify assumptions
* Explore alternative problem interpretations

AI output should be treated as **hypotheses to investigate**, not established facts.

---

## 2. Users and Personas

AI can assist with:

* Persona drafts
* User needs
* User scenarios
* Possible pain points
* User journey ideas

The team should validate whether these assumptions make sense for the actual product context.

Ask:

> **Do we have evidence that this represents a real user need?**

---

## 3. Product Vision and Goals

AI can help explore:

* Product Vision alternatives
* Product objectives
* Desired outcomes
* Value propositions
* Success measures

The Product Owner selects and refines the final direction.

AI should not determine product strategy independently.

---

## 4. User Stories

AI can help transform requirements into structured User Stories.

For example:

``
As a [user]
I want [capability]
So that [value]
```

AI can also help identify:

* Missing acceptance criteria
* Edge cases
* Ambiguous requirements
* Alternative interpretations

The team must review every generated story for relevance and clarity.

---

## 5. Product Backlog

AI can assist with:

* Breaking large requirements into smaller stories
* Suggesting backlog items
* Grouping related items
* Identifying dependencies
* Finding duplicate requirements
* Suggesting prioritisation questions

However:

> **AI may suggest priority. The Product Owner owns priority.**

---

## 6. Product Review

AI can also help prepare for Sprint Reviews by:

* Summarising progress
* Comparing outcomes with goals
* Identifying missing acceptance criteria
* Generating review questions
* Analysing feedback

The actual product evidence remains the source of truth.

---

## Responsible Product Use

Do not use AI to:

* Invent user research and present it as real research.
* Manufacture customer feedback.
* Claim validation that never occurred.
* Replace conversations with actual users.
* Make unsupported business claims.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **AI can expand the product team's thinking. It cannot replace product judgement.**

The Product Owner remains accountable for **value, priority and product direction**.
