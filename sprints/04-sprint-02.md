# Sprint 2 — Product Definition

> **Turn problem understanding into a focused product direction and actionable backlog.**

Sprint 2 builds on Sprint 1.

The team now moves from **"We understand the problem"** to **"We know what valuable product outcome we are trying to achieve."**

---

## Sprint Goal

> **Define the Product Vision, Product Goals and initial Product Backlog.**

---

## Inputs

Bring forward:

* Sprint 1 Problem Statement
* Users / Personas
* Initial Product Direction
* Key Assumptions
* Sprint 1 feedback

Do not restart the product discovery process.

---

## 00–10 min — Plan

The team should:

* Review Sprint 1 outcomes.
* Confirm the most important user/problem.
* Identify the desired product outcome.
* Decide what must be defined during this Sprint.

The Product Owner leads product prioritisation.

---

## 10–45 min — Execute

Create the following.

### 1. Product Vision

Write a concise statement describing:

* Who the product serves
* What it helps them achieve
* The value it intends to provide

### 2. Product Goals

Define a small number of measurable or observable outcomes.

Avoid creating a long list of features.

### 3. Initial User Stories

Create the most important User Stories.

Use:

``
As a <user>,
I want <capability>,
so that <value>.
```

Add simple Acceptance Criteria where appropriate.

### 4. Product Backlog

Organise the stories and other known work into an initial backlog.

Prioritise the items.

> **Do not try to specify the entire product.**

Focus on what is needed to create the first valuable increment.

---

## 45–55 min — Review

The team presents:

1. Product Vision
2. Product Goals
3. Top User Stories
4. Initial Backlog
5. Highest-priority item

The team should be able to explain:

> **What should we build first, and why?**

---

## 55–60 min — Retrospective

Discuss:

* What became clearer?
* Which requirement is still uncertain?
* Did we create too much scope?
* Is the highest-priority work genuinely valuable?
* What should change in Sprint 3?

Record one improvement.

---

## Sprint Deliverables

By the end of Sprint 2:

* [ ] Product Vision
* [ ] Product Goals
* [ ] Initial User Stories
* [ ] Acceptance Criteria
* [ ] Prioritised Product Backlog
* [ ] Sprint Review evidence
* [ ] One Retrospective improvement

---

## Definition of Done for Sprint 2

Sprint 2 is Done when the team can clearly answer:

> **What product are we building, what outcome matters most, and what should we build first?**

### Sprint Principle

> **A focused backlog is more valuable than a complete-looking backlog.**
