# Operating Model

> **A common operating model enables different teams to work consistently while building different products.**

The AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint uses one common operating model for all project teams.

The technology, product domain and implementation may differ, but the way the team organises and executes its work remains consistent.

---

## 1. Operating Model

``
PRODUCT
  ↓
PRIORITY
  ↓
SPRINT
  ↓
ENGINEERING
  ↓
WORKING INCREMENT
  ↓
REVIEW
  ↓
FEEDBACK
  ↓
BACKLOG UPDATE
  ↺
```

This is a continuous operating loop.

---

## 2. Product Ownership

The **Product Owner** maintains the product perspective.

Primary responsibilities include:

* Understanding the problem
* Representing user needs
* Maintaining product priorities
* Clarifying User Stories
* Managing the Product Backlog
* Accepting or rejecting completed work

The Product Owner answers:

> **What should we build next, and why?**

---

## 3. Sprint Facilitation

The **Scrum Master** protects the Agile working process.

Responsibilities include:

* Facilitating Sprint activities
* Keeping the team focused
* Identifying impediments
* Protecting time-boxes
* Encouraging collaboration
* Supporting Retrospectives

The Scrum Master answers:

> **How can the team work more effectively?**

---

## 4. Engineering

The Engineer / DevOps Engineer and other technical team members turn product requirements into working solutions.

Responsibilities include:

* Technical analysis
* Solution design
* Implementation
* Integration
* Testing
* Deployment
* Technical decisions

The engineering team answers:

> **How can we build and operate it reliably?**

---

## 5. Collective Team Responsibility

Although responsibilities are assigned to roles, the product belongs to the entire team.

Team members should:

* Communicate frequently
* Share knowledge
* Help remove blockers
* Review each other's work
* Integrate continuously
* Support the Sprint Goal

Avoid the pattern:

``
Person A → "My task"
Person B → "My task"
Person C → "My task"
```

Prefer:

``
             SPRINT GOAL
                  ↓
          ┌───────┴───────┐
          │      TEAM     │
          └───────┬───────┘
                  ↓
          WORKING PRODUCT
```

---

## 6. Decision Flow

Important decisions should happen close to the work.

``
QUESTION
   ↓
UNDERSTAND
   ↓
OPTIONS
   ↓
DECISION
   ↓
IMPLEMENT
   ↓
VALIDATE
```

Significant technical or product decisions should be recorded so that the team can explain **what was decided and why**.

---

## 7. Feedback Loop

Feedback enters the system continuously.

Sources may include:

* Users
* Product Owner
* Sprint Review
* Testing
* Engineering observations
* Retrospectives
* New information

Feedback should influence priorities rather than being treated as criticism of the team.

---

## Operating Principle

> **Product decides value. Agile organises the work. Engineering creates the solution. The team learns and improves continuously.**
