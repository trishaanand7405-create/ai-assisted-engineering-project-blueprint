# AI-Assisted Engineering Overview

> **AI assists engineering. Engineers remain responsible for the product.**

AI-assisted engineering means using Artificial Intelligence as a supporting capability throughout the product lifecycle.

The objective is not to replace engineering judgement.

The objective is to help teams **think, build, test and learn faster while maintaining quality and accountability**.

---

## 1. Where AI Can Assist

AI may support activities such as:

* Product discovery
* Research
* Problem analysis
* User-story refinement
* Solution exploration
* Architecture discussion
* Coding
* Debugging
* Testing
* Documentation
* Data analysis
* Technical review

The appropriate use depends on the task and the available tools.

---

## 2. The Human Responsibility

AI-generated output must be treated as **assistance, not authority**.

The engineering team remains responsible for:

* Understanding the requirement
* Making decisions
* Reviewing AI output
* Testing results
* Protecting data
* Checking assumptions
* Evaluating risks
* Accepting or rejecting generated content

Use this principle:

``
AI SUGGESTS
    ↓
ENGINEER REVIEWS
    ↓
ENGINEER VALIDATES
    ↓
TEAM DECIDES
    ↓
PRODUCT USES
```

---

## 3. AI Across the Lifecycle

AI can participate throughout the product lifecycle:

``
DISCOVER
   ↓
DEFINE
   ↓
DESIGN
   ↓
BUILD
   ↓
TEST
   ↓
RELEASE
   ↓
LEARN
   ↓
IMPROVE
```

The role of AI may change at each stage.

It should always remain aligned with the current product objective.

---

## 4. Responsible Use

Teams should:

* Never blindly copy AI-generated code.
* Never assume AI output is correct.
* Validate important technical claims.
* Avoid exposing confidential information.
* Understand generated code before using it.
* Test generated solutions.
* Record important AI-assisted decisions when useful.

AI should reduce unnecessary effort—not reduce engineering discipline.

---

## 5. AI as a Team Capability

AI assistance should be available to the whole team.

The Product Owner may use AI for product analysis.

The Scrum Master may use AI for facilitation and planning.

Engineers may use AI for design, coding and testing.

The team should share useful discoveries and reusable techniques.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **Use AI to increase engineering capability, not to outsource engineering responsibility.**

A strong AI-assisted engineering team can explain:

**what it built, why it built it, how it built it, how it validated it, and where AI assisted the process.**
