# Sprint 5 — Integration & Testing

> **Connect the pieces, test the product and discover what actually works.**

Sprint 5 focuses on integration and validation.

The team should move beyond individual components and verify that the **complete product flow** works as intended.

---

## Sprint Goal

> **Integrate the implemented components and produce evidence that the product increment satisfies its acceptance criteria.**

---

## Inputs

Bring forward:

* Sprint 4 working increment
* Selected User Story
* Acceptance Criteria
* Solution Design
* Testable inputs and expected outputs
* Known limitations

---

## 00–10 min — Plan

The team should:

* Identify the critical end-to-end flow.
* Review the Acceptance Criteria.
* Identify the most important tests.
* Assign testing and integration responsibilities.
* Decide what evidence will be demonstrated.

Prioritise tests that provide the greatest confidence.

---

## 10–45 min — Execute

### 1. Integrate

Connect the major components.

For example:

``
INPUT
  ↓
PROCESSING
  ↓
CORE LOGIC
  ↓
OUTPUT
```

The exact flow depends on the Project Track.

### 2. Test

Test the important behaviours.

Consider:

* Normal inputs
* Invalid inputs
* Boundary conditions
* Expected outputs
* Failure conditions

### 3. Fix

Identify and resolve the most important defects discovered during testing.

Do not attempt to eliminate every possible defect.

Focus on defects that affect the Sprint Goal.

### 4. Record Evidence

Capture simple evidence such as:

* Test results
* Screenshots
* Output examples
* Logs
* Metrics
* Demonstration results

---

## 45–55 min — Review

Demonstrate the end-to-end flow.

The team should show:

1. Input
2. Processing
3. Core capability
4. Output
5. Test evidence

Then explain:

* What passed?
* What failed?
* What was fixed?
* What remains?

> **A successful test result is evidence — not an assumption.**

---

## 55–60 min — Retrospective

Discuss:

* Which integration problem surprised us?
* Which test was most valuable?
* What defect taught us something important?
* What should we test earlier next time?
* What should change in Sprint 6?

Record one improvement.

---

## Sprint Deliverables

By the end of Sprint 5:

* [ ] Integrated product flow
* [ ] Acceptance Criteria reviewed
* [ ] Critical tests executed
* [ ] Important defects addressed
* [ ] Test evidence recorded
* [ ] Known limitations documented
* [ ] End-to-end demonstration
* [ ] One Retrospective improvement

---

## Definition of Done for Sprint 5

Sprint 5 is Done when:

> **The critical product flow works end-to-end and the team has evidence supporting that claim.**

A product that works only in isolated components is **not yet an integrated product**.

### Sprint Principle

> **Integrate early. Test realistically. Trust evidence, not assumptions.**
