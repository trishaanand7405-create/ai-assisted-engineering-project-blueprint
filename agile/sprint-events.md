# Sprint Events

> **Every Sprint is a short, disciplined cycle of planning, execution, feedback and improvement.**

The team uses four core events during each **1-hour Sprint**:

1. Sprint Planning
2. Sprint Execution
3. Sprint Review
4. Sprint Retrospective

The team must respect the Sprint time-box.

---

## 1. Sprint Planning — 10 Minutes

### Purpose

Decide **what the team will accomplish** during the Sprint and how it will approach the work.

### Team Activities

**Product Owner**

* Explains the highest-priority backlog items
* Clarifies requirements
* Confirms acceptance criteria

**Scrum Master**

* Facilitates the discussion
* Keeps the team focused
* Helps establish the Sprint Goal

**(DevOps) Engineer + Team**

* Assesses technical feasibility
* Identifies dependencies and risks
* Breaks work into actionable tasks
* Agrees on the Sprint Backlog

### Output

```text
Sprint Goal
+
Sprint Backlog
+
Initial Task Allocation
```

---

# 2. Sprint Execution — 35 Minutes

### Purpose

**Build the Sprint Increment.**

The team designs, develops, integrates and tests the selected functionality.

Team members should:

* work collaboratively
* communicate frequently
* keep work visible
* test as they build
* resolve impediments quickly
* avoid unnecessary work outside the Sprint Goal

The Scrum Master monitors progress and helps remove impediments.

The Product Owner remains available to clarify product questions.

### Output

**Working Product Increment**

---

# 3. Sprint Review — 10 Minutes

### Purpose

Inspect the increment and obtain feedback.

The team demonstrates the **working product**, not merely slides or source code.

### Demonstration Sequence

```text
Requirement
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
Test
    ↓
Working Result
```

The Product Owner / stakeholders evaluate the increment against the agreed expectations.

Discuss:

* What was completed?
* What was not completed?
* Does it satisfy the acceptance criteria?
* What feedback was received?
* What should change next?

### Output

**Accepted work + Feedback + Updated Backlog**

---

# 4. Sprint Retrospective — 5 Minutes

### Purpose

Improve **the way the team works**.

The team quickly discusses:

### What went well?

Identify practices worth continuing.

### What did not go well?

Identify problems, delays or communication issues.

### What will we change?

Select at least **one concrete improvement** for the next Sprint.

### Output

**Improvement Action**


## Event Discipline

During the workshop:

* Start and finish on time.
* Keep discussions focused.
* Demonstrate working results.
* Record important decisions.
* Convert new requirements into backlog items.
* Do not allow one technical problem to consume the entire Sprint.
* Ask for help when blocked.

> **Time-boxing creates focus. Feedback creates learning. Retrospectives create improvement.**
