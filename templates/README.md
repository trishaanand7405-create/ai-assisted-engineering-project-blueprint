# Templates

> **Reusable structures for creating consistent engineering and product artefacts.**

This folder contains lightweight templates for the recurring artefacts used throughout the AI-Assisted Engineering Project Blueprint.

The templates are intentionally adaptable. They provide a useful starting structure without attempting to prescribe every project-specific detail.

---

## Template Lifecycle

The templates correspond to recurring activities across the engineering lifecycle:

```text
PROJECT
   ↓
PRODUCT
   ↓
USER NEED
   ↓
BACKLOG
   ↓
SPRINT
   ↓
ENGINEERING DECISION
   ↓
DELIVERY
   ↓
INCIDENT / LEARNING
   ↓
RETROSPECTIVE
   ↺
```

They therefore complement the conceptual and procedural material elsewhere in the blueprint.

---

## How to Use the Templates

A template should be:

1. Copied or adapted for the specific project.
2. Filled with project-specific information.
3. Reviewed by the appropriate people.
4. Maintained as the work evolves.
5. Archived or superseded when no longer applicable.

Templates are **starting structures**, not mandatory paperwork.

---

## Templates and Engineering Judgement

A template should never become a substitute for thinking.

Teams may:

* Remove irrelevant sections.
* Add project-specific sections.
* Combine related sections.
* Simplify the template.
* Create specialised variants when justified.

The objective is consistency where consistency provides value.

## Relationship with Checklists

The `checklists/` folder answers:

> **"Are we ready?"**

The `templates/` folder answers:

> **"How should we structure the artefact we need to create?"**

Together:

```text
CHECKLIST
    ↓
READINESS
    ↓
TEMPLATE
    ↓
ARTEFACT
    ↓
REVIEW
    ↓
DECISION
```

## AI-Assisted Template Usage

AI can assist with:

* Filling initial drafts
* Identifying missing information
* Summarising source material
* Structuring notes
* Converting discussions into candidate artefacts
* Reviewing completeness

However, AI-generated content should be reviewed before becoming an authoritative project artefact.

> **A completed template is not automatically a valid engineering decision or project record.**

The responsible team remains accountable for its contents.

---

## Template Design Principles

The templates in this folder follow several principles:

### Lightweight

They should be usable without excessive administrative overhead.

### Evidence-Oriented

Important claims and decisions should be supported by appropriate evidence.

### Adaptable

Templates should work across different projects and team contexts.

### Traceable

Important decisions should be understandable later.

### Human-Owned

AI may assist with preparation, but people remain responsible for decisions and commitments.

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

Do not use this folder as a repository for:

* Project-specific documents
* Completed project records
* Large procedural manuals
* Detailed technical specifications
* Team-specific private notes

Those belong in the appropriate project or engineering workspace.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **Templates create consistency without creating bureaucracy.**

Their purpose is to make good engineering practices easier to repeat, easier to understand and easier to maintain.
