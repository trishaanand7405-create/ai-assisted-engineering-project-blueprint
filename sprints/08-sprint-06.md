# Sprint 6 — Working Product

> **Turn the integrated technical solution into a coherent, usable product increment.**

Sprint 6 focuses on **product usability, completeness and demonstration readiness**.

The team should now evaluate the product from the user's perspective rather than only from the engineer's perspective.

---

## Sprint Goal

> **Create a coherent working product that delivers the intended user outcome.**

---

## Inputs

Bring forward:

* Integrated product from Sprint 5
* Product Vision
* Product Goals
* User Stories
* Acceptance Criteria
* Test evidence
* Known defects and limitations

---

## 00–10 min — Plan

The team should identify:

* The primary user journey.
* The most important product capability.
* Remaining defects affecting usability.
* What must be ready for demonstration.
* What can safely remain outside the current scope.

Prioritise the **user experience and product outcome**.

---

## 10–45 min — Execute

Improve the working product.

Activities may include:

### Product Experience

* Improve the user flow.
* Simplify confusing interactions.
* Improve messages and outputs.
* Remove unnecessary steps.

### Technical Integration

* Fix remaining integration issues.
* Improve reliability.
* Handle important errors.
* Refine data/model/agent/simulation behaviour.

### Demonstration Readiness

Prepare a realistic scenario that shows:

``
USER
  ↓
PRODUCT
  ↓
CAPABILITY
  ↓
RESULT
  ↓
USER VALUE
```

The demonstration should tell a coherent product story.

---

## 45–55 min — Review

Conduct a **Product Review**.

Demonstrate:

1. The user/problem
2. The product
3. The primary workflow
4. The resulting outcome
5. Supporting evidence

Then ask:

> **Does this actually solve the problem we identified in Sprint 1?**

---

## 55–60 min — Retrospective

Discuss:

* What improved for the user?
* What still feels incomplete?
* What technical debt did we create?
* What should be fixed before release?
* What should change in Sprint 7?

Record one improvement.

---

## Sprint Deliverables

By the end of Sprint 6:

* [ ] Coherent working product
* [ ] Primary user journey
* [ ] Major usability issues addressed
* [ ] Important defects addressed
* [ ] Demonstration scenario
* [ ] Supporting evidence
* [ ] Known limitations
* [ ] One Retrospective improvement

---

## Definition of Done for Sprint 6

Sprint 6 is Done when:

> **A user can experience the primary product journey and understand the value being delivered.**

The product does not need to be feature-complete.

It must be **coherent, usable and demonstrable**.

### Sprint Principle

> **A working system becomes a product when it delivers a meaningful outcome to a user.**
