## 3. Backlog Prioritisation Principles

Prioritisation should consider:

* User value
* Product value
* Risk reduction
* Learning value
* Dependencies
* Urgency
* Cost or effort
* Technical sustainability

Do not prioritise work solely because it is technically interesting.

## 8. Ready for Sprint

Items selected for upcoming Sprint consideration should satisfy the appropriate readiness expectations.

* [ ] User or stakeholder need is understood.
* [ ] Expected value is clear.
* [ ] Acceptance Criteria are available.
* [ ] Dependencies are understood.
* [ ] Important assumptions are visible.
* [ ] Item is sufficiently sized.
* [ ] Required design or research is available.
* [ ] Testing expectations are understood.

---

## 9. Backlog Refinement

During refinement:

* [ ] Remove obsolete items.
* [ ] Clarify ambiguous items.
* [ ] Split oversized items.
* [ ] Update priorities.
* [ ] Identify dependencies.
* [ ] Identify risks.
* [ ] Add Acceptance Criteria.
* [ ] Reconsider assumptions.
* [ ] Add newly discovered work.

---

## 10. AI-Assisted Backlog Management

AI may assist with:

* Grouping similar backlog items
* Identifying duplicate items
* Suggesting User Story decomposition
* Identifying missing Acceptance Criteria
* Summarising backlog themes
* Highlighting potential dependencies
* Suggesting prioritisation questions

AI should **not independently determine product priorities**.

Human product ownership remains responsible for prioritisation and trade-offs.

---

## 11. Backlog Health

Periodically review:

* [ ] Is the backlog aligned with the Product Goal?
* [ ] Are the highest-value items clearly visible?
* [ ] Are obsolete items removed?
* [ ] Are large items being decomposed?
* [ ] Are important technical items represented?
* [ ] Are risks visible?
* [ ] Is the backlog becoming unnecessarily large?
* [ ] Can the team identify what should be worked on next?

## 13. Backlog Metrics

Use metrics only when they support useful decisions.

Possible measures include:

* Number of high-priority items
* Age of backlog items
* Items completed per Sprint
* Defect trends
* Technical-debt trends
* Time from idea to delivery

Avoid treating metrics as goals by themselves.
